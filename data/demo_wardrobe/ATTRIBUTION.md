# Demo wardrobe image attribution

Images in `images/` were selected by hand, checked visually, and verified against the Stage 1 vision model
(category, colour and slot) before inclusion. Images were resized to at most 800 px; one Pexels image was cropped to the garment.
Images in `store/` are silhouettes generated by `fashion_pipeline.utils.image_converter` and carry no third-party rights.
